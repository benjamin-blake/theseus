## What decided the big calls

**Workflow outputs (82 percent of the affected population) are exempt on edit-pattern grounds, not consumer tier.** Tracing the producing and consuming workflows: plans are produced whole per run and revised in-session by the critique loop; the only post-merge agent edits are narrow field flips (`/implement`'s graduate-to-waive disposition flip, closes_criteria bookkeeping); audits are write-once; OVERSEER ledgers take per-slice status appends. No agent performs the whole-file comprehension edit NS-A protects. A cap would wedge `/plan` and `/audit` outright -- this audit's own YAML deliverable measures 820 effective lines, which is the self-referential check passing honestly: the deliverable exceeds 500, its class is exempt under this audit's own verdict, and nothing was trimmed or split to dodge that conclusion. The disclosed residuals: `audits/` has no schema gate, and the class contains the repository's most extreme byte-per-line outlier (the OVERSEER report: 326 effective lines, 255 KB, an 8,211-character line).

**The census rule survives contact with reality, with one caution.** Every grounding-map number reproduced (87 over-500 vs 86 at composition -- one plan of drift; per-extension byte ratios to the decimal). The pinned Python counting rule miscounts nothing in the current tree: all 33 tracked `.tf` files use `#` comments exclusively (zero `//` lines, zero block comments), and JSON has no comments. The per-language risk is prospective, and one lesson from this audit's own measurement pass is recorded in Q2: a regex-based block-comment stripper was fooled by `/*` inside AWS ARN strings -- correct per-language counting needs a lexer, which argues for keeping the simple pinned rule plus a line-comment-prefix extension, not clever per-language parsing.

**The harm is assumed, and the audit says so.** No CI-RCA or recommendation attributes any defect to model comprehension failure on a large non-Python file. The nearest evidence: executor-era recs rec-242/rec-285 (size-triggered escalation to Sonnet -- for Python), and two incidents of agent-authored malformed refs in large plan YAML caught by schema gates. This capped every finding below critical and sized Q6 as calm grandfathering rather than urgent waves.
